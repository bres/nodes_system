Nodes Management Documentation

This application manages physical locations, organizational units, users, devices, peripherals, device assignments, and material handover reports.

Physical Locations
City Area Building Floor Room Space Socket and/or Device
City Area Building Floor Common Area Socket and/or Device

A Room belongs to a Floor and contains Spaces. A Common Area belongs directly to a Floor. Sockets and Devices must each be assigned to either a Space or a Common Area, but not both.

Organization Structure
General Directorate Directorate Department Office User

The organization hierarchy is General Directorate → Directorate → Department → Office. User records can reference these organizational units.

Devices and Peripherals
Device (shared device fields)
└─ Device types:
Desktop Computer All-in-One Computer Laptop Computer Server Computer Printer Switch Access Point UPS Phone
└─ Peripherals belong to one Device:
Peripherals (keyboard, monitor, mouse)

Device types share common fields such as brand, model, serial number, usage type, location, and optional socket. Peripherals are linked to a Device.

  • A Device must have exactly one location: a Space or a Common Area.
  • A Device may optionally use a Socket. The Socket must be in the same Building, and a Socket can be assigned to at most one Device.
  • Serial numbers are unique. Registration codes, IP addresses, and MAC addresses must also be unique when provided.
Deletion Behavior and Dependencies
RESTRICT
Deletion is blocked while a protected child record still references the item. Remove or reassign those references first.
CASCADE
Deleting the parent also deletes related records whose relationship uses CASCADE.
SET NULL
The related record remains, but its optional reference to the deleted item is cleared.
What happens when you delete a record?
  • City: blocked while it has Areas. Area: blocked while it has Buildings. Building: blocked while it has Floors. These links use RESTRICT.
  • Floor: deleting it cascades to its Rooms and Common Areas. This also removes their Spaces, Sockets, Devices, and any Assignments or Peripherals belonging to those Devices.
  • Room: deleting it cascades to its Spaces and their dependent Sockets and Devices. Space or Common Area: deleting either cascades to its Sockets and Devices.
  • Socket: its Devices are kept; each Device's optional Socket reference is cleared (SET_NULL).
  • General Directorate: blocked while Directorates or Users still reference it (RESTRICT).
  • Directorate, Department, Office: deletion cascades to their dependent organizational records and Users. Deleting a User also deletes that User's Assignments.
  • Device: deletion removes its device-type record, Peripherals, and Assignments.
  • Peripheral or Assignment: can be deleted individually without deleting its parent Device or User.

A delete blocked by RESTRICT shows an error; remove or reassign the remaining references before trying again. Cascaded deletions remove child records permanently.

Lifecycle Execution & Validation Rules
  • Records using lifecycle fields have an active flag and valid-from/valid-to dates. Saving a record outside its date window marks it inactive; it does not delete the record.
  • City names are unique without regard to case. Area names are unique within a City; Building names within an Area; Room codes within a Floor; Space names within a Room; and Common Area names within a Floor.
  • Floor numbers must be at least -2 and unique within their Building.
  • Socket codes are unique within a Building. Device serial numbers are unique; registration codes, IP addresses, and MAC addresses are unique when provided.
  • General Directorate names and organizational names within their parent level must be unique. User email addresses are unique without regard to case.
Recommended Record Creation Order

Create parent records before the records that depend on them.

  1. Locations: City → Area → Building → Floor, then Room → Space or Common Area.
  2. Organization: General Directorate → Directorate → Department → Office, then User.
  3. Devices: create a Socket or Device at a Space/Common Area; add optional Peripherals to a Device.
  4. Assignments: link an existing User and Device with assignment dates and type.
Assignment Rules
  • The Assignment form rejects end dates earlier than assignment dates.
  • If a Device has a shared assignment, its other assignments must also be shared; shared and primary assignments cannot coexist.
  • Primary assignment periods cannot overlap for the same Device or User. A User's primary and temporary assignments for the same Device cannot overlap.
  • Material handover reports require selected Users to have an Assignment whose date range includes today, and to share the same single Device.
Material Handover Report

The report form requires at least one User. Each selected User must have an Assignment whose date range includes today, and all selected Users must share the same single Device. The form also records a report date and optional comments.
